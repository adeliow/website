The problem

We close the gaps schools can't see

Schools have resources and schools have programmes, but they are rarely connected into one wellbeing system. Bee Well doesn't add more programmes, we connect what already exists.

Our system

The Sustainable School Framework

Four areas, assessed and delivered together, adding up to one thing: school well-being.

Student well-being

Mental health support, counselling capacity and the habits that hold it together.

Safe environment

Physical safety, air, water and the spaces students spend their day in.

Healthy school culture

Staff, parents and students pulling in the same direction, not past each other.

Sustainable system

Waste, consumption and transport, run so the school can keep running it.
