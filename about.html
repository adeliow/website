Where we started

Founded to fix a coordination problem

Schools rarely lack care. What they lack is a way to see all of it at once — the counsellor, the canteen, the sports programme, the pastoral team — and to know whether it adds up to a student actually being well.

Bee Well started as consulting work with a handful of Jakarta schools and grew into a single assessed framework that a school can run year after year, rather than a series of one-off workshops.

How we work

What a year with Bee Well looks like

We assess first and prescribe second. Every engagement follows the same four stages, so schools know what they are committing to before they start.

01

Assess

We audit what the school already runs across all framework areas, and survey students and staff.

02

Map

The audit becomes a single picture of strengths, gaps and overlaps, presented to school leadership.

03

Build

We design the year's programme around the gaps, using the school's existing people and resources first.

04

Review

We re-assess at the end of the cycle, so the school can see movement rather than assume it.
